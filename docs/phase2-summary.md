# Phase 2 teaching experience

## Scope delivered

Phase 2 turns the initial 3D engine proof of concept into a fuller demonstration-course experience while retaining the safety gate that prevents unreviewed material from being presented as formal instruction.

- searchable and filterable course library;
- versioned local favourites and learning progress;
- course overview, prerequisites, equipment and learning objectives;
- step completion checklists;
- operation-hand cues and rope-head direction cues;
- contact, risk and inspection markers in the 3D scene;
- optional common-error rope-path comparison;
- 2D SVG fallback for weak devices or WebGL failures;
- a more anatomically legible, fully clothed procedural adult female training mannequin;
- automatic recommended-camera following that can be disabled;
- updated unit and desktop/mobile browser tests.

## Publication status

The demonstration course remains `prototype-only`, non-load-bearing and non-suspension. The procedural character is an engineering placeholder, not a production human asset. Neither the course nor the character may be marked approved until the required rope-technique, anatomy/safety and asset reviews are complete.

## Next quality gates

1. Replace the procedural mannequin through the documented, licensed production-asset adapter.
2. Independently review every pose, rope path, direction cue and release order.
3. Record reviewer identity, review date, findings and course/model versions.
4. Validate GLB topology, rigging, weights, LODs, compressed textures and mobile memory use.
5. Only then evaluate additional body ties; high-risk or load-bearing content remains out of scope.
